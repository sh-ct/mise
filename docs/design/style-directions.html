How these settings would work in the app

  • AppearanceAlways light, always dark, auto by time of day (dark from 7pm to 7am, or sunset if we have location), or auto by device theme. Library and cook mode follow the same setting.
  • Big typeStep text about 40% larger and the next-step footnote hidden, so the step is all you see from across the kitchen.
  • Stand modeFor a tablet or phone on a stand in landscape: step on the left, its ingredients on the right, nothing to scroll.
  • Step photosOn phones, a round camera button between Back and Next (only on steps that have a photo) opens the photo in a sheet over the lower half, keeping the step text visible. Turning the phone sideways, or using a tablet or desktop, switches to stand mode with the photo beside the step. Library photos are always shown.
